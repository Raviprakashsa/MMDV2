MMD V2 - Product Requirements Document (PRD)
1. Executive Summary
MMD V2 is a White Label SaaS platform designed for Recruitment Agencies, Staffing Companies, HR
Consultancies, and Talent Acquisition Teams.
The platform aims to centralize recruitment operations, automate repetitive tasks, improve recruiter
productivity, provide real-time reporting, and eliminate dependency on spreadsheets, disconnected tools,
and manual workflows.
The system will support multiple organizations (tenants) under a single platform while allowing complete
branding customization for each customer .
2. Vision
To become a complete Recruitment Operations Platform that helps staffing companies manage their entire
recruitment lifecycle from lead acquisition to placement and invoicing through a single unified system.
3. Mission
Provide recruitment companies with a scalable, automated, secure, and customizable platform that
increases recruiter productivity, reduces operational overhead, and improves placement success rates.
