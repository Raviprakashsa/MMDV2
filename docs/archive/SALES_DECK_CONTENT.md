## Slide 2: The Problem
- **Header:** The Recruitment Workflow Crisis
- **Bullet Points:**
  - **Siloed Systems:** Client relationships are tracked in one software, while candidates live in another, causing manual data entry errors.
  - **Data Leakage Risks:** Shared databases expose private candidate resume info and corporate account records across teams.
  - **Weak Operations Pipeline:** Opportunities (Leads) slip through the cracks due to unstructured pipelines and lack of validation.

---

## Slide 3: The Solution
- **Header:** One Unified Workspace. Complete Security.
- **Bullet Points:**
  - **Single Platform:** Merges ATS and CRM workflows cleanly.
  - **Tenant Isolation:** Multi-tenant database design prevents any cross-client data leak.
  - **Rigid FSM Opportunities:** Opportunities progress only through a validated, linear pipeline to guarantee process control.

---

## Slide 4: Key Features
- **Header:** Powering Your Placement Velocity
- **Bullet Points:**
  - **ATS Core:** Sourcing, job postings, interactive resume storage, and interview scheduling.
  - **CRM Core:** Companies directory, contact cascade deactivations, and FSM-driven Leads tracker.
  - **IAM Security:** 5 distinct granular roles mapped to business functions (`SUPER_ADMIN` to `SCRAPER`).

- **Header:** Concrete Business Impact
- **Bullet Points:**
