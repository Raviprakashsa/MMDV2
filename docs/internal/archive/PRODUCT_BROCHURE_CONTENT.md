## 2. Product Introduction
MMD Recruit CRM is the next-generation workspace for recruitment professionals. By combining the strengths of a robust Applicant Tracking System (ATS) with a highly secure Client Relationship Management (CRM) engine, MMD Recruit CRM eliminates system fragmentation. Your team can source candidates, launch job pages, track interview feedback, manage client accounts, and advance sales opportunities in a single, high-performance interface.

---

## 3. Core Feature Summaries

### 💼 Unified CRM Module
Manage client opportunities with high precision.
- **Cascading Contacts:** Update client accounts with automated status overrides for associated contacts.
- **Validated Leads Engine:** Opportunities follow a strict, verified progression (NEW → CONTACTED → QUALIFIED → PROPOSAL → WON/LOST) so deals are never updated incorrectly.

### 👥 Applicant Tracking (ATS)
Accelerate screening, interviewing, and hiring.
- **Interactive Job Pages:** Automatically publish active roles to public candidates.
- **Slick Resumes Management:** Clean uploads and indexing to search candidates.
- **Calendar Scheduling:** Align recruiters, interviewers, and candidates with scheduled events.

### 🔒 Enterprise Isolation & Control
Enterprise-level security for boutique firms.
- **Multi-Tenant Architecture:** Total database isolation between workspaces.
- **Role-Based Access:** Standardized role policies ensuring Recruiter and Scraper accounts only see what they need.

## 4. Key Advantages
- **Fast Performance:** Next.js architecture compiles into high-speed static pages and server-rendered dashboards.
- **Demonstrated Reliability:** Backed by automated backup crons, Sentry diagnostic integrations, and secure Docker persistence.
